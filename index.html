Community roots. Cultural pride. Lasting impact.

Founded in 2004, ICCHA connects and supports Indian-origin families in Canada through leadership programs, youth mentorship, heritage preservation, and public service.

Education

Mentorship that builds confident youth

We create pathways for student growth through mentorship, recognition, and community-led learning.

Heritage

Stories and traditions preserved in Canada

Our archive and cultural programming protect shared history and celebrate identity across generations.

Recognition

Awards that honour service and leadership

Each year we spotlight changemakers whose contributions strengthen communities in Manitoba and beyond.
