Objectives and values of the association

The India Canada Cultural & Heritage Association serves people of Indian origin that have settled in Canada.

01

Education

Mentorship & academic development

We build student success through mentorship programs, recognition of youth excellence, and pathways that strengthen leadership and confidence.

02

Heritage

Preserving cultural memory in Canada

We preserve community history through curated archives, landmark initiatives, and documented stories that connect generations with dignity.

03

Achievement

Recognizing contribution and excellence

We celebrate distinguished contributions in civic life, health, sports, and community service while inspiring ongoing participation and impact.
