Objectives and values of the association

The India Canada Cultural & Heritage Association serves people of Indian origin that have settled in Canada.

🎓

Education

We strive for promoting and mentoring education; recognizing academic excellence of young people; promoting racial, religious and cultural harmony people of Indian origin.

🏛️

Heritage

We document stories, milestones, and historical records of Indian settlement in Manitoba, including community heritage moments such as the Gandhi statue efforts.

🏅

Achievement

We recognize accomplishments of people and serve to highlight their contributions to Canada; encouraging people to participate in sports and health related activities.
