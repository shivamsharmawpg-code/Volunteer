History of the India Canada Cultural & Heritage Association

India Canada Culture and Heritage Association (ICCHA) Inc. was established in 2004 as a non-profit, non-political, non-religious organization with unique aims and objectives concerning human rights and education.

ICCHA has established a lifetime achievement award presented yearly. This highest honour is bestowed upon individuals who have made outstanding achievements in their field of expertise and/or profession over years of leadership as well as dedication to the welfare of society.

The first such award in 2004 was presented to Her Excellency Shashi Tripathi, then the High Commissioner of India to Canada. ICCHA also raised $125,000 for the Canadian Museum for Human Rights.
