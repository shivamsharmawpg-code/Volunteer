# indiaheritage.ca local clone

This repository contains the static website for **ICCHA Canada** — the **India Canada Cultural and Heritage Association Inc.**

ICCHA is a non-profit organization dedicated to strengthening education, human rights, Indian cultural heritage, mentorship, awards, and community leadership in Manitoba and across Canada.

## Website overview

- Website: `https://indiaheritage.ca/`
- Organization: India Canada Cultural and Heritage Association Inc. — ICCHA
- Founded: 2004
- Location focus: Manitoba and Canada
- Core themes:
  - Indian cultural heritage preservation
  - Education
  - Youth mentorship
  - Community leadership
  - Awards and scholarship recognition
  - Public service
  - Human rights support

### Lifetime Achievement Award

Status:

- Applications closed.

Description:

This is ICCHA’s highest honour. It is bestowed upon individuals who have made outstanding achievements in their field of expertise and/or profession over years of leadership and dedication to the welfare of society.

The award may include:

- A plaque
- A cash prize, depending on resources

Eligibility:

- Individuals of any nationality or religion
- One or more awards may be given during a year, subject to approval of the ICCHA Board

Nomination and selection:

- Nominations may be obtained through advertisement and/or from members of the ICCHA Executive Committee.
- An Awards Committee with at least five members, including a chairperson, is appointed for selection.

Evaluation criteria:

- Merit and impact of achievements
- Recognition and profile of the individual
- Overall interests of ICCHA

### Distinguished Service Award

Status:

- Applications closed.

Description:

This award is bestowed upon individuals who have provided highly valuable and meritorious services in their profession and/or area of expertise for the welfare of society.

The award may include:

- A plaque
- A cash prize, depending on resources

Eligibility:

- Individuals of any nationality or religion
- One or more awards may be given during a year, subject to approval of the ICCHA Board

Nomination and selection:

- Nominations may be obtained through advertisement and/or from members of the ICCHA Executive Committee.
- An Awards Committee with at least five members, including a chairperson, is appointed for selection.

Evaluation criteria:

- Merit and impact of services
- Recognition of services
- Overall interests of ICCHA

### High School & University Student Scholarship Awards

Status:

- Applications open for the 2026 cycle.

ICCHA established annual awards for High School Students and Post-Secondary Institute Students to encourage youth of Indian origin to achieve excellence in academics and sports.

### What runs on this website

The site is published as static files on GitHub Pages.

The website does not run:

- A server-side application
- User accounts
- Login system
- Shopping cart
- Advertising pixels
- Payment processing

The website uses or links to:

- GitHub Pages hosting
- Google Analytics 4
- Google Fonts
- FormSubmit for the contact form
- Email links
- Facebook

### Google Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website and improve content.

GA4 may collect:

- IP address
- Device type
- Browser type
- Pages visited
- Approximate location
- Clicks
- Navigation paths
- Visit dates
- Visit times

Google Analytics opt-out link:

- `https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout`

### Cookies and consent

Google Analytics may set analytics cookies or use similar identifiers.

ICCHA does not use these cookies to run advertising on the website.

The site currently relies on implied consent rather than a pop-up cookie banner because it uses basic analytics and provides a visible Privacy Policy with an opt-out method.

Visitors who do not want Google Analytics cookies can:

- Use Google’s opt-out browser add-on
- Adjust browser settings
- Avoid using the site

### GitHub Pages hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

GitHub may receive technical information such as:

- IP address
- Browser details
- Device information
- Request dates and times
- Security or service logs

GitHub privacy statement:

- `https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement`
