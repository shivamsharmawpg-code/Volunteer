Privacy Policy

This page explains how ICCHA Canada handles information when you visit indiaheritage.ca, which is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It is intended to be practical and easy to understand, not legal advice.

Effective date: July 22, 2026
Last updated: July 22, 2026

What runs on this website

This website is published as static files on GitHub Pages. We do not run our own server-side application, user accounts, login system, shopping cart, advertising pixels, or payment processing on this site.

The site does use a few third-party services and links that can receive technical information from your browser: GitHub Pages hosting, Google Analytics 4, Google Fonts, FormSubmit for the contact form, email links, and links to Facebook.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand how people use this website. GA4 helps us see which pages are useful, how visitors move through the site, and how we can improve the website.

GA4 may collect usage data such as your IP address, device type, browser type, pages visited, approximate location, and on-site behavior such as clicks, navigation paths, visit dates, and visit times.

This analytics data is shared with Google for analytics purposes. Google may process the data on our behalf and may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies to provide measurement services.

You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on from Google: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Cookies and consent

Google Analytics may set analytics cookies or use similar identifiers. These cookies help us measure visits and improve the website; they are not used by ICCHA Canada to run advertising on this site.

Because this Manitoba-based site uses basic analytics and provides this visible Privacy Policy with an opt-out method, we currently rely on implied consent rather than a pop-up cookie banner. If you do not want Google Analytics cookies, please use the opt-out link above, adjust your browser settings, or avoid using the site.

GitHub Pages hosting

The website is hosted entirely on GitHub Pages. When you visit the site, GitHub may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser details, device information, request dates and times, and related security or service logs to deliver and protect the website.

GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Contact forms, email, and documents

If you use the contact form, your form submission is sent to ICCHA Canada through FormSubmit at formsubmit.co. The form asks for your name, email address, and message. FormSubmit and the email providers involved may process your submission and related technical metadata so the message can be delivered.

If you email us directly, register for an event, apply for a program, submit a membership form, or send documents, we may receive the information you choose to provide. This can include your name, email address, message, registration details, application details, payment confirmations, biographies, photographs, PDFs, or other files needed to respond to you or manage ICCHA programs.

The site also links to downloadable PDFs and uploaded images. Your browser requests those files from the website host when you open or download them.

Other third-party services and links

  • Google Fonts: this site loads the Inter font from Google Fonts, so your browser may make requests to Google font domains.
  • Facebook: this site links to ICCHA Canada’s Facebook page. If you click that link, Facebook may receive information about your visit and handle it under its own policies.
  • External opt-out link: the Google Analytics opt-out link goes to Google’s website.

How we use information

  • To respond to questions, messages, membership inquiries, and volunteer interest.
  • To manage events, awards, scholarships, mentorship, memberships, and community programs.
  • To improve website content, navigation, accessibility, performance, and outreach.
  • To protect the website, troubleshoot technical issues, and keep appropriate administrative records.

Privacy questions and requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to ask what information ICCHA Canada may have about you, or want to request a correction or deletion where appropriate, email us at iccha.mb@gmail.com or use our contact page.

We may need enough information to identify the relevant records, and some information may need to be kept for legal, governance, security, or administrative reasons.

Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, programs, analytics tools, service providers, or legal obligations change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are posted.
