Award & Student Scholarship Awards

Student award applications are now closed.
All awards will be presented at the ICCHA Gala dinner in Fall of 2026.

Lifetime Achievement Award - applications closed

This highest honour will be bestowed upon individuals, who have made outstanding achievements in their field of expertise and/or profession over years of leadership as well as dedication to the welfare of society. Such an award in the form of a plaque and cash prize (depending upon resources) will be given to individuals of any nationality or religion at one or more times during a given year subject to approval of the ICCHA Board.

For this purpose, nominations will be obtained upon appropriate advertisement and/or from members of the ICCHA Executive Committee. An Awards Committee with at least 5 members including a chairman will be appointed for selection of the Awardee.

The Awards Committee will evaluate each nominee in terms of the following criteria:

  • Merit and impact of achievements
  • Recognition and profile of the individual
  • Overall interests of ICCHA

Distinguished Service Award - applications closed

This prestigious award will be bestowed upon individuals, who have provided highly valuable and meritorious services in the area of their profession and/or expertise for the welfare of society. Such awards in the form of a plaque and cash prize (depending on resources) will be given to one or more individuals of any nationality or religion during a given year subject to approval of the ICCHA Board.

For this purpose, nominations will be obtained upon appropriate advertisement and/or from members of the ICCHA Executive Committee. An Awards Committee with at least 5 members including a chairman will be appointed for selection of the Awardee.

The Awards Committee will evaluate each nominee in terms of the following criteria:

  • Merit and impact of services
  • Recognition of services
  • Overall interests of ICCHA

High School & University Student Scholarship Awards - applications closed

ICCHA has established annual awards for High School Students and Post-Secondary Institute Students, to encourage youth of Indian origin to achieve excellence in academics and sports. Applicants must also include a one-paragraph description of future goals and a 500-word essay on their significant achievements. Submit any supportive material along with the application package.

Applications for the current cycle are closed. Please check back for the next application window.
