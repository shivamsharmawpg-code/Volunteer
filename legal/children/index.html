Children's Privacy

Last updated: May 26, 2026.

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without appropriate parental involvement.

Our approach

  • Minimize child personal-data collection.
  • Escalate youth program intake processes for parental/guardian handling where needed.
  • Remove data submitted in error by or about children when requested.

If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.
