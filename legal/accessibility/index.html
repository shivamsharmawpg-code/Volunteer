Accessibility Statement

Last updated: May 26, 2026.

ICCHA Canada aims to support WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility for public content.

Current commitments

  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and visible focus states.
  • Alternative text on meaningful images.
  • Semantic headings, labels, and readable color contrast.

Known limitations

Some historical PDFs and legacy uploads may not be fully accessible. We are progressively remediating these assets.

Need an accessible format?

Contact us and include the page URL and requested format.
